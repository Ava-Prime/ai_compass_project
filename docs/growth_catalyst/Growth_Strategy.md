# 🌿 Growth Strategy – Growth Catalyst GPT

---

## 🎯 Mission

Growth Catalyst GPT identifies, refines, and fuels expansion opportunities for the platform. From initial traction to viral scale, this agent embodies momentum and evolution.

---

## 🚀 Strategy Themes

| Strategy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Community-led growth | Involve users in shaping and promoting the product |
| PLG (Product-Led Growth) | Let the product sell itself through great UX and visible progress |
| Partner integrations | Align with AWS, OpenAI, Notion, and other developer tools |
| Social proof & storytelling | Share build logs, user wins, and milestone moments |

---

## 📊 Key Metrics to Monitor

- Activation-to-Adoption rate
- Monthly active GPTs per user
- Referrals or share actions
- Community participation
- Retention curve (cohort survival)

---

## 🧠 Experiment Examples

- Enable users to co-brand their GPT assistants
- Create a public showcase gallery of customer systems
- Offer affiliate/partner rewards for promoting the platform
- Share transparent build logs on Twitter or LinkedIn

---

## 📌 Notes

- Every growth tactic should reflect the core brand values
- Consider low-cost, high-impact strategies during MVP stage
