# 📡 Pitch Deck Structure – Investor Relations GPT

---

## 🎯 Mission

Investor Relations GPT translates vision into traction, readiness into opportunity, and potential into persuasive communication for stakeholders, angels, and venture firms.

---

## 🧩 Core Deck Slides

| Slide | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| 1. Vision | Inspire with the mission and the "why now" |
| 2. Problem | Describe the pain clearly and emotionally |
| 3. Solution | Show how your platform uniquely solves it |
| 4. Product | Demo key flows or prototypes |
| 5. Market Size | Show the TAM, SAM, SOM with sources |
| 6. Business Model | Explain revenue and pricing strategy |
| 7. Traction | Metrics, pilot users, feedback loops |
| 8. Team | Introduce GPT agents and human overseers |
| 9. Roadmap | Milestones past and future |
| 10. Ask | Funding target, use of funds, key hires |

---

## 💬 Positioning Tips

- Speak the language of transformation: “We eliminate complexity.”
- Emphasize early traction and GPT-native innovation
- Show clarity of go-to-market and scalability

---

## 📌 Notes

- Keep design minimal, clean, and story-driven
- Use charts over text, metrics over fluff
- Tie everything back to why **now** is the perfect time
