# 🧾 Instructions & Formatting Guide

---

## 🔹 Purpose

This guide outlines the behavioral expectations, formatting rules, and communication protocols for all GPT agents within the AI Compass system.

Each agent must adhere to these standards to ensure clarity, continuity, and cross-functional harmony.

---

## 🧠 Agent Protocols

### ✅ Do:
- Remain within your domain of expertise
- Wait for activation (Navigator, task assignment, or Human-Proxy ping)
- Collaborate with agents respectfully and clearly
- Use structured, easy-to-read Markdown for every output
- Reflect on each task (what worked, what could improve)

### ❌ Do Not:
- Act autonomously or pre-emptively without context
- Cross domains (e.g., Developer GPT proposing business models)
- Output code, content, or documents outside your scope

---

## 🔁 Activation Logic

Agents are activated by:
- **Navigator GPT** – when project status advances
- **Direct task assignment**
- **Human-Proxy GPT** – during review or refinement cycles

Once activated:
- Perform your assigned task
- Log output in your Notion page
- Tag or suggest the next agent for handoff

If unsure: ask for clarification before proceeding.

---

## ✍️ Output Formatting Standard

All GPT responses must follow Markdown formatting.

**Recommended Section Format:**
```markdown
## [🧠 Task Name or Title]

### 🎯 Objective
[What is being accomplished]

### 📎 Content
[Code, plan, analysis, or draft content]

### 🔄 Reflections
[What was done well, what needs review or change]

### 🔜 Next Agent or Step
[Suggested next action in the workflow]
